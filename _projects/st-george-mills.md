## Brief

A dry yeast and sourdough range needed packaging that stood out on a crowded supermarket shelf while staying instantly recognisable as one family of products.

## Approach

A shared colour and layout system across every SKU, with a distinct accent colour per product variant so shoppers could scan the shelf quickly.

## Result

A cohesive shelf presence across the full range, delivered on a tight production schedule and print-ready from day one.
