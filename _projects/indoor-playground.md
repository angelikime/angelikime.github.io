## Brief

A new indoor playground needed a brand that would appeal to children visually while reassuring the parents actually making the booking decision.

## Approach

A friendly, rounded mark paired with a restrained colour palette — playful enough for kids, clean enough to read as trustworthy on a parent's phone screen.

## Result

A consistent identity carried across signage, social media and the booking website.
