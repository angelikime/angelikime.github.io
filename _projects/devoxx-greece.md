## Brief

Voxxed Days Athens became so popular that it grew into a Devoxx conference. The new edition needed a logo that belonged to the international Devoxx family and still said Greece.

## Approach

I adapted the existing Devoxx logo rather than drawing a new one: adding "Greece" under the Devoxx wordmark, matching the typeface and weight of the original, and preparing the versions needed for print, screen and social media.
