## Brief

A brand that has to travel. Every year, the same identity needs to work across a new venue, a new city, sometimes a new country — badges printed by a local vendor, banners hung by a different crew, stage graphics run by whoever is on shift that day.

## Approach

A kit, not a one-off. Instead of redesigning from scratch each year, I built a flexible system: a fixed type and colour scale, a badge template that adapts to any lanyard printer, and banner templates sized for whatever the venue actually has.
